# Editing for Understanding

## Follow the explanation without rewinding

Read the narration continuously, without relying on the writer's background knowledge. For each section, check:

- Is each technical term explained before it is used to support another idea?
- Can the viewer identify who acts, what information they have, and why the next step follows?
- Does the example supply the necessary domain context?
- Are the user requests plausible, and the outcomes causally consistent?
- Are observed behavior, illustrative cases, and proposed designs distinct?

If the answer is no, add the missing explanation or reorder the material. Do not fix confusion by merely repeating the same paragraph in simpler words.

## Make it speakable

Use familiar vocabulary around necessary technical terms. Prefer active voice and concrete actors. Split nested clauses, but keep connected prose instead of turning every sentence into a slogan. Remove tangents and repeated conclusions after the explanation works.

Do not invent names for frameworks the viewer must learn just to understand this video. Introduce identifiers as labels for an already understood purpose. Replace broad metaphors with a concrete example when the metaphor creates another concept to explain.

## Verify the visual track

Check the script against its visual cues:
- A process appears in its real order, one active step at a time.
- Labels distinguish external messages from actions performed by the local program.
- Narration points to what matters in code or a payload and interprets it afterward.
- The screen supports the current spoken idea rather than introducing competing dense text.
- A viewer has time to inspect the visual before it disappears.

Avoid file paths, version strings, internal module tours, and long payload inventories unless they are necessary to the lesson.

## Check evidence and scope

A successful test demonstrates the tested decision, not universal improvement. Name the scope in everyday language and put detailed test metadata in production notes. Verify counts, claims, and provider-specific distinctions against sources. Do not narrate a hypothetical failure as a reproduced incident.

## Final pass

Read aloud from hook to ending. Check that the opening promise is fulfilled, transitions answer a natural next question, and the ending supplies a usable takeaway. Count narration words separately from notes and estimate duration with an explicit speaking-rate range plus visual pauses.
